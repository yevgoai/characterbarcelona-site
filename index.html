Questions

Before your
first visit.

The things people ask us most often. Can’t find your answer? Send us a message.

How do I book and choose a barber?+

Tap “Book a visit” and our Altegio booking page opens. Pick a barber (or “Any specialist”), a service and a time that works for you.

What is included in a men’s haircut?+

We offer classic men’s cuts, skin fades, tapers and longer styles. Your visit includes a consultation, wash, haircut, styling and care advice. The haircut appointment is 60 minutes; for longer hair, choose the dedicated service in Altegio.

Can I come with my son?+

Of course — bring him along! We’ll book you both with two barbers at the same time, so nobody has to wait. Message us on WhatsApp and we’ll sort it out.

I am not sure which service to choose.+

Message us on WhatsApp with the length and look you’re after — feel free to send a photo of a cut you like. We speak English, Spanish, Ukrainian and Russian, and Yevhen and Anastasia speak Polish too.
